Aldo Coppola is a Milanese hair brand founded by Maestro Aldo Coppola, the creator of Live Show — the first hair fashion show, held in Milan since 1983. In Moscow the brand runs full-service beauty centres: haircuts and styling, the signature Shatush colouring, natural herbal colouring Infusion, cosmetology, nails, brows and make-up. Up to six services can be done at the same time in one visit.
